About AECC

The Asociación Estudiantil de Ciencias de Cómputos (AECC) is a student-led organization at the University of Puerto Rico, Río Piedras Campus (UPRRP). Established to represent computer science students, the organization works towards fostering a community of collaborative coding, hosting workshops, and providing tutoring services.

Our Mission

To prepare students for professional careers in computing disciplines by hosting technical seminars, networking events, coding bootcamps, and providing resources to excel academically and technically.

Our History

For years, the AECC has been the hub of innovation in the Computer Science department. From sending students to local hackathons (like HackPR) to conducting specialized research talks and building open-source projects, our members actively shape the technical landscape of UPRRP.
