Frequently Asked Questions

What is the AECC RADIUS System?

The RADIUS system controls student device authentication for our local private student network. It ensures that only validated student organization members can route internet traffic, utilizing MAC address validation.

How do I register my device?

Go to the Link Device portal, input your registered Student ID and telephone number, and the portal will automatically detect your device's MAC address and authorize it in the RADIUS databases.

Why is my device MAC address blocked?

Devices can be administratively sandboxed / blocked due to expired memberships or network policy violations. If you suspect an error, visit the Computer Science department lab or contact an admin.

What are the network policies?

Members get high-speed internet permissions. Default guests are redirected to validation. Sandboxed units are restricted to DNS/routing blocks to safeguard intranet environments.
